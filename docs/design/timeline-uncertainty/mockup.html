Between Two Dates

Over half the Models of Authority clause corpus is dated to a range — 1235 × 1265, circa 1170 × 1174 — and the search timeline throws that away, plotting every charter at the earliest year it could possibly be. Three ways to give the uncertainty back, each drawn below from the live index.

What the timeline drops today

Ignored

date_max is never read. The chart is built from facetDistribution.date_min alone, so a charter that could be anywhere in a 30-year window is drawn as a spike on its first year.

Phantom

A “0s” bar opens the chart. 191 undated clauses carry date_min = 0 and render as a first-century decade, ahead of the 1090s.

Truncated

163 clauses never reach the chart. Meilisearch caps facet values at 100; the corpus has 108 distinct date_min values, and the overflow is dropped silently.

Ships today

Earliest year only

Every charter counted once, at date_min. A range of 1214 × 1249 lands entirely in the 1210s and says nothing about the 35 years after it.

Reads as
False precision. The peaks are artefacts of where ranges begin, not where charters are.
Cost to change
One line — use-search-page-state.ts:139 already has date_max in the same payload.
Option 1

Spread the weight

Each charter contributes 1 ÷ span to every year it could belong to. A 1235 × 1265 charter puts 1/31 of itself in each of 31 years. Totals still sum to the corpus, so the axis stays a count.

Reads as
Where the corpus probably sits. Honest shape, no invented spikes, and the total is conserved.
Trade-off
Bars become fractional — no decade means “N charters” any more, and a click-to-filter needs its own count.
Option 2

Certain and possible

Two stacked segments per decade. Solid: charters whose whole range falls inside it — these are certainly here. Pale: charters whose range merely overlaps it — these might be. The floor is a guarantee; the ceiling is the outer limit.

Certainly in this decade Possibly in this decade
Reads as
“At least 40, at most 260.” Keeps whole charters, so every segment is still a real count you can filter on.
Trade-off
The pale band double-counts — one charter appears in every decade it touches, so the ceilings don’t sum to the corpus.
Option 3

Span ribbons

Abandon the histogram. One horizontal ribbon per distinct dating, sorted by its earliest year — 290 of them across the corpus. Point-dated charters are ticks; wide ranges are long bars. The uncertainty is the picture.

Reads as
The editorial reality of the corpus: dense certainty around the great charters, long shallow ranges either side.
Trade-off
Not a distribution — you can’t read “how many” off it, and it needs real height. Best as a companion to 1 or 2, not a replacement.

Whichever way this goes

The two smaller defects are worth fixing in the same pass regardless: undated records should be excluded from the axis and reported beside the chart (“191 undated”), not drawn as year 0; and the timeline should stop being built from a capped facet distribution, which is silently losing 163 clauses today.
